Mobile App Reccommendations

Here are some of the best mobile applications that may help increase productivity, efficiency, and enhance skils.

  • 1. Notion

    • Notion is great for online note taking and to-do list making. It organizes all your pages and has many efficient features.

  • 2. Forest

    • Forest is an app that can be used for focus while studying. The longer you study, your forest grows bigger. It's a calm and inspiring app to help students study.

  • 3. Canva

    • Canva can be used to design and create presentations, posters, and more. This is useful for students who need to create and imagine.
